Expennytics for iPhone

Track spending, sheet by sheet.

Give every budget its own sheet, mark bills as pending until they’re paid, and see exactly where your money goes. Synced privately through your own iCloud.

  • Sheets, not spreadsheets. One sheet per budget, each with its own balance and currency.
  • Quick entry. Type amounts as math, pick categories with one tap, duplicate or move entries in bulk.
  • Clear stats. Breakdowns by category and payment method for any period.
  • Multi-currency. Every common currency, with per-sheet exchange rates.
  • Private by design. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your data lives on your device and in your iCloud.
