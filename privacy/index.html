Expennytics

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Expennytics is an expense tracking app for iPhone made by Nam Nguyen (“the developer”). This policy explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and what it never does.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your device and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own private iCloud account.
  • The developer has no server and no access to your data.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

Everything you enter in the app is stored so the app can work:

  • Sheets: name, currency, pinned and archived state, trash state.
  • Entries: type, amount, currency, category, payment method, merchant, note, dates, trash state.
  • Categories, payment methods, the list of currencies you use, and per-sheet exchange rates.
  • Preferences: theme, primary color, and whether iCloud Sync is on.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any identifier, and it does not collect device or usage information.

Where your data lives

On your device. All data is kept in a local database inside the app’s own storage on your iPhone. It is covered by the device’s built-in encryption and by your passcode or biometric lock.

In your iCloud, only if you choose. iCloud Sync is off by default. When you turn it on, the app copies your data to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit service, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can see it. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. Only you, through your Apple Account, can read it. The developer cannot see, download, or restore it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

When iCloud Sync is on, the app registers for silent push notifications so that changes from your other devices arrive promptly. These notifications carry no content and are never shown to you.

CSV import

When you import a CSV file, the app reads it on your device to create entries. The file is not uploaded anywhere, and the app does not keep a copy of it.

What the developer does not do

  • No collection of personal data.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services.
  • No advertising or ad identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No third-party software development kits.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting a sheet or an entry moves it to the app’s Trash, where you can recover it or delete it forever.
  • Deleting the app removes the local database from your device.
  • To remove synced data from iCloud, open the iOS Settings app, go to your Apple Account, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, find Expennytics, and delete its data. Turning iCloud Sync off in the app stops future uploads but does not delete data already in iCloud.

Children

Expennytics is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub.
